## Coverage logic

A balanced reviewer set should cover at least three distinct parts of the manuscript rather than asking one person to review every layer:

```text
A  evolutionary / adaptive-dynamics theory
B  eco-evolutionary / conceptual ecology synthesis
C  finite-population / stochastic / population-genetic realization
```

One possible **coverage pattern**, subject to conflict screening, is:

```text
A  Michael Doebeli
B  Florence Débarre or Hanna Kokko
C  Lindi Wahl or Joshua Plotkin
```

This is not a ranking and should not be copied into Editorial Manager until conflicts have been checked.

## Mandatory author-side conflict screening

Before suggesting any person, the authors must confirm at least:

- no collaboration with any author during the journal's stated recent-conflict window (currently previous 48 months);
- no current employment at the same institution as any author;
- no close adviser/advisee, personal, financial, or other relationship that could reasonably impair independence;
- no other journal-specific conflict disclosed by Editorial Manager.

If a candidate fails any check, remove them rather than trying to justify the conflict.
