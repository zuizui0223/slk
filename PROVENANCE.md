# SLK Provenance

SLK was initialized as an integration repository on 2026-09-10 from four public sister repositories owned by `zuizui0223`:

- `sch`
- `balance`
- `bita`
- `payoff`

The integration is conceptual and claim-level. SLK does not silently copy empirical evidence or convert source-repository theoretical quantities into measurements.

## Imported core ideas

From SCH:
- shared-coordinate conflict budget `L`;
- causal-identification caveat for compromise geometry.

From BALANCE:
- persistent-compromise classification `L>0, Phi<0`;
- interpretation of the middle world as real conflict without profitable differentiation.

From BITA:
- recoverable architecture benefit `R`;
- general architecture margin `Phi=R-K`;
- quadratic partial-release corollary `R=sL`, hence `Phi=sL-K` under the registered assumptions;
- partial differentiation as partial recovery.

From PAYOFF:
- global-value versus local-accessibility distinction;
- canonical frequency-dependent transport of architecture value;
- separation of invasion, fixation criteria, and weak-mutation occupancy;
- reciprocal-fixation / stationary-occupancy ordering invariant under the registered symmetric rare-mutation exponential-Moran process.

## Non-imported programmes

The following remain canonical in the sister repositories unless explicitly generalized in SLK:

- SCH contextual/pure-function optimum identification and empirical crossed-design programme;
- BALANCE worldline geometry, depth/reserve/topology and persistence methods;
- BITA ecological mechanism-identification workflow, route synthesis, and full robustness/generalization programme beyond the minimum architecture bridge;
- PAYOFF continuous branching, modular topology, spatial spectral, and temporal Floquet theory.

## Operational boundaries

`R=sL` is never treated as a universal identity in SLK. It is a model-specific quadratic corollary whose assumptions remain traceable to BITA.

`K` is never imported as an unexamined biological residual. Any empirical SLK use must declare comparison states, fitness scale, time horizon, included and excluded cost channels, overlap checks with `R`, and uncertainty or bounds as specified in `docs/K_OPERATIONAL_DEFINITION_V1.md`.

## Citation rule

Any future manuscript should cite the archived/versioned source repositories or their eventual papers for derivations and empirical assets that originated there. SLK is the canonical home only for the integrated hierarchy, its split/invariant synthesis, and the cross-level empirical claim ladder.
