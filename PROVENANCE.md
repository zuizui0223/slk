## Operational boundaries

`R=sL` is never treated as a universal identity in SLK. It is a model-specific quadratic bridge whose assumptions are now registered and owned in SLK; historical derivation provenance does not transfer the `Phi=0` boundary back to BITA.

`K` is never imported as an unexamined biological residual. Any empirical SLK use must declare comparison states, fitness scale, time horizon, included and excluded cost channels, overlap checks with `R`, and uncertainty or bounds as specified in `docs/K_OPERATIONAL_DEFINITION_V1.md`.

A direct worldline contrast and decomposed `Phi` are identified only under a declared common-world bridge. Any residual mismatch remains diagnostic until independently explained.
