## Main reviewer attack now expected

> The threshold atlas is exact, but is the composite model too deliberately constructed to reveal the desired separations?

The response should be precise rather than defensive:

1. the model is deliberately minimal because the contribution is an estimand handoff, not a universal mechanistic model of every route to differentiation;
2. each added coordinate has a distinct biological meaning — release geometry, frequency feedback, finite-population process, mutation process;
3. the model does not force universal separation: the same construction produces an exact re-alignment at Phi=0;
4. every surface has an empirical gate, so the framework makes different measurements necessary rather than merely renaming concepts;
5. the manuscript explicitly states where the mapping fails, especially unequal same-type feedback, non-Moran fixation, non-rare/asymmetric mutation, and alternative mutation paths.

## Remaining technical boundaries that must stay visible

### Equal-diagonal-feedback mapping

The pre-interaction endpoint architecture margin equals the canonical population-game Phi only under the registered mapping in which same-type ecological feedback does not shift the endpoint contrast. If H_DD differs from H_SS, the population self-play gap must be adjusted.

### Small-step accessibility

The k=k_local result concerns sufficiently small selectively uphill release along a declared path. It does not prove that D is historically unreachable by drift, a large mutation, recombination, or a different developmental route.

### Absolute fixation boundary

3Phi=eta is retained only as the registered weak-selection criterion. It must not be presented as an exact strong-selection surface.

### Occupancy

The Gibbs-like monomorphic occupancy law requires the rare-mutation substitution limit and the registered reversible/symmetric mutation assumptions.

## Empirical risk

No same-system G1-G9 receipt exists. This remains the main ceiling on biological generalization. The theoretical upgrade improves the logic and presentation but does not raise the empirical claim ceiling.

The strongest future upgrade remains a same-system G1-G5 result that independently measures conflict, recoverable benefit, architecture cost, and Phi, ideally followed by a prospectively predicted crossing or a sharp persistent-compromise case.

An Ecology Letters reassessment still requires real same-system empirical closure rather than further theoretical ornamentation.
