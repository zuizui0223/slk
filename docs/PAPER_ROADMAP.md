# SLK Publication Architecture

## A-tier publication programme

### Flagship — SLK

Working title:

**From functional conflict to evolutionary architecture: when does differentiation pay, and when does payoff become evolution?**

Primary target: **The American Naturalist**.

Core spine:

```text
L -> R -> Phi -> accessibility -> invasion -> fixation -> occupancy
                                                + INV1
```

Core claims:

1. `L` separates multifunctionality from identified shared-coordinate conflict.
2. `L>0, Phi<0` is a real persistent-compromise state, not absence of conflict.
3. `Phi=R-K` is the architecture-value crossing, with `R=sL` retained only as the quadratic partial-release bridge.
4. `Phi>0` does not imply local evolutionary accessibility.
5. architecture value, rare invasion, fixation, and long-run occupancy are distinct estimands.
6. under the registered symmetric rare-mutation exponential-Moran process, reciprocal fixation ordering and stationary monomorphic occupancy ordering re-align exactly (`INV1`).

Flagship exclusions:

- contextual-versus-pure-function optimum identification details;
- full middle-world certification machinery, reserve/depth/topology and hysteresis;
- post-differentiation ecological route identification;
- continuous architecture and branching;
- edgewise modularization and topology theory;
- general spatial spectral transport;
- temporal Floquet theory beyond what is required for the flagship spine.

The flagship owns the cross-repository estimand transport and claim-ceiling logic, not every technical extension developed in the source repositories.

### Full paper 1 — SCH

Working thesis:

**Multifunctionality is not evidence of functional conflict.**

Independent novelty:

- state-specific optima are not automatically pure-function optima;
- a crossed intervention plus component contrasts supplies an explicit promotion gate;
- multifunctionality alone does not identify opposing causal geometry;
- the paper ends at identified conflict / compromise and exports `L` to SLK rather than re-deriving the downstream architecture programme.

Canonical distinction:

```text
multifunctionality
    !=
identified functional conflict
```

Best route: conceptual-methodological ecology paper with empirical qualification/validation programme.

---

### Full paper 2 — BITA

Working thesis:

**Trait interaction is not ecological mechanism.**

Primary target: **Ecology — Concepts & Synthesis** (or a comparable methodology/conceptual venue).

Independent novelty:

- a positive trait interaction identifies nonadditivity, not the biological route that generated it;
- identified sets, partial identification, selective intervention and separability diagnostics provide a mechanism-identification ladder;
- the source-adjudicated synthesis of 56 route records / 25 independent biological clusters and the 17-system high-information frontier documents recurrent constituent biology but fragmented identification;
- the paper does not claim the SLK architecture-value crossing as its own central theorem.

Canonical distinction:

```text
trait interaction
    !=
identified ecological mechanism
```

### Short paper / Note — PAYOFF-B

Working thesis:

**Anti-phase environmental switching generates an exact finite-migration optimum.**

Scope is deliberately narrow:

- exact two-patch/two-season anti-phase solution;
- the temporal premium relative to the static mean system;
- existence and uniqueness of the finite optimum `u*` / scaled migration optimum;
- asymptotic constants and threshold structure only insofar as they support that one result.

Do not load this Note with continuous architecture, general topology theory, generic spectral metapopulation theory, or the full PAYOFF transport hierarchy.

Best route: short mathematical-biology / theoretical-ecology Note.

## B-tier technical modules / dormant paper branches

The following are **not in the active publication queue**. They remain citable, versioned technical modules and should be archived with a Zenodo DOI when stable.

### BALANCE certification module

Retain:

- two-sided middle-world certificate `L>0, Phi<0`;
- direct optimized-worldline identification;
- position `xi`, depth `d_B`, reserve `rho`;
- width/connectivity/topology diagnostics;
- direct-versus-decomposed concordance;
- no-reentry sufficient condition;
- switching-cost hysteresis/persistence.

Current status: technical substrate for SLK, not a standalone manuscript in the active queue.

Reactivation rule: a standalone BALANCE paper can be reconsidered if direct empirical worldline data recover a nontrivial middle-world geometry or if a genuinely independent theorem family emerges that cannot be reduced to SLK's `L>0, Phi<0` state classification.

### PAYOFF-A continuous-architecture module

Retain:

- interior architecture optimum;
- branching threshold;
- endpoint-coexistence closure;
- accessibility barriers.

Current status: DOI module / dormant branch.

Reactivation rule: reconsider only if the continuous theory acquires an independent empirical target or a stronger theorem that is not merely a continuous extension of the flagship binary contrast.

### Spatial spectral module

Retain:

- local-to-metapopulation transport through the principal eigenvalue;
- source-sink rescue and migration thresholds;

Current status: DOI module / dormant branch.

Reactivation rule: reconsider with a distinctive empirical spatial system or a theorem beyond standard spectral transport that materially changes the biological conclusion.

### Topology / edgewise modularization module

Current status: DOI module / dormant branch.

## Ownership rule

The publication programme is organized by **independent scientific question**, not by repository count.

```text
SCH       asks whether conflict has actually been identified.
SLK       asks what must happen before identified conflict becomes evolutionary architecture.
BITA      asks whether an observed interaction has actually identified its ecological mechanism.
PAYOFF-B  asks one sharp dynamical question about anti-phase environmental switching.
```

Technical modules can be cited by the papers without being promoted to independent manuscripts.

## Anti-duplication rule

1. SCH may export `L` but does not own downstream `Phi -> accessibility -> population transport` claims.
2. SLK may use the existence of the middle world but does not absorb BALANCE's full certification machinery.
3. BITA may retain `R`, `s`, and `Phi` as upstream definitions, but its independent paper centers on mechanism identification rather than re-claiming the architecture-value crossing.
4. PAYOFF-B does not re-present the full architecture transport hierarchy; it starts from the minimal dynamical setup needed for the anti-phase exact result.
5. Dormant branches remain versioned and citable. They are not failed papers and can be reactivated only when an independent question or decisive empirical anchor appears.
