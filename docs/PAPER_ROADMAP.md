# SLK Paper Roadmap

## Flagship paper — SLK

Working title:

**From functional conflict to evolutionary architecture: when does differentiation pay, and when does payoff become evolution?**

Core claims:

1. `L` separates multifunctionality from identified shared-coordinate conflict.
2. `L>0, Phi<0` is a real persistent-compromise state, not absence of conflict.
3. `Phi=sL-K` is the architecture-value crossing.
4. `Phi>0` does not imply local evolutionary accessibility.
5. architecture value, rare invasion, fixation, and long-run occupancy are non-equivalent estimands.

Flagship exclusions:

- contextual-versus-pure-optimum identification details;
- middle-world depth/reserve/topology;
- post-differentiation ecological route identification;
- continuous branching;
- full topology theory;
- spatial spectral and temporal Floquet extensions.

## SCH residual paper

Working thesis:

**Causal identification of functional conflict in multifunctional traits.**

Primary novelty:

- state-specific optima are not automatically pure-function optima;
- a crossed intervention plus component contrasts supplies a promotion gate;
- multifunctionality alone is not evidence of opposing causal geometry.

Best route: conceptual-methodological paper plus empirical qualification/validation programme.

## BALANCE residual paper

Working thesis:

**How to identify and quantify persistent compromise.**

Primary novelty:

- direct optimized-worldline route to the middle world;
- middle-world depth, reserve, width, topology and deepest-point geometry;
- concordance between direct and decomposed architecture comparisons;
- optional hysteresis/persistence extension.

Best route: methods/theoretical ecology paper.

## BITA residual paper

Working thesis:

**Trait interaction is not ecological mechanism.**

Primary novelty:

- interaction detection does not identify the route generating fitness gain;
- identified sets, partial identification, selective intervention and separability diagnostics provide a mechanism ladder;
- empirical route synthesis shows recurrent biology but fragmented identification.

Best route: Concepts & Synthesis / methodology paper.

## PAYOFF residual paper A

Working thesis:

**From partial modularity to branching and discrete architecture.**

Primary novelty:

- continuous recovery coordinate;
- interior architecture optimum;
- branching threshold;
- edgewise recovery derivative;
- conditions under which continuous coupling produces vertex/topological solutions.

## PAYOFF residual paper B

Working thesis:

**Spatial and temporal transport of architecture payoff.**

Primary novelty:

- local static margins become spectral metapopulation invasion;
- common temporal forcing is a null;
- source switching generates a noncommutative temporal premium;
- exact two-patch/two-season solution and finite migration optimum.

## Development rule

The flagship should become narrower as sister papers become stronger. Sister papers should develop orthogonal estimands or stronger generalizations rather than re-claiming the same central theorem.
