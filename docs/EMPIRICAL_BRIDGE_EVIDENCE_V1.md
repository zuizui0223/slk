# SLK empirical bridge evidence V1

## Purpose

This ledger asks how far existing public or openly described empirical systems connect the three early SLK decision layers in one biological system.

The three layers are architecture/collective value, evolutionary accessibility, and rare establishment. No literature example is promoted to an estimate of Phi, g0, or Delta_R unless the estimand actually matches the SLK definition.

## Result

No public system identified in the current screen closes the full ordered SLK persistence turnover from architecture-value bottleneck to path bottleneck to rare-establishment bottleneck across environments while retaining the same integrated architecture.

Two systems nevertheless provide complementary empirical anchors.

## A. Pedicularis rex — natural selection mosaic around a persistent integrated architecture

### What is directly supported

Sun, Armbruster & Huang (2016) measured floral traits, stigmatic pollen loads, initial seed set, seed predation and final viable seed production across 12–14 field populations. Greater corolla exsertion is associated with greater pollen receipt and also greater seed predation, while seed-predator-mediated selection varies geographically more strongly than the pollinator side.

Sun & Huang (2015) experimentally manipulated water held by the cupular bracts. Across six field populations, background seed predation ranged from 1.36% to 27.42%; draining bracts increased seed predation without changing legitimate pollinator visitation.

Xia, Sun & Liu (2013) found a density-dependent predator effect over two years. Patch density did not significantly affect fruit set or initial seed set, whereas sparse patches had lower final seed set and greater fruit and seed predation. The raw data are publicly archived on Dryad as raw data.xlsx, DOI 10.5061/dryad.6cv06.

### What this gives SLK

P. rex is a strong natural-context anchor: documented functional conflict, persistent integrated morphology, and large geographic/density variation in the antagonist environment.

It supports the premise that an unchanged integrated architecture can occupy substantially different ecological selection contexts.

### What it does not give SLK

The density treatment concerns density of the resident P. rex population. It is not a manipulation of the frequency of a structurally divided alternative.

Resident-density Allee effect is therefore not Delta_R of a divided architecture.

The existing data cannot identify the fitness of a specified divided floral architecture relative to integration (Phi), the local selection gradient along a structural-release path (g0), or invasion fitness of a rare divided architecture (Delta_R). The 2013 Dryad data are valuable for ecological-context analysis but cannot close the SLK establishment gate by reinterpretation.

## B. Pseudomonas fluorescens — experimental positive control when the early gates are permissive

Kim, Levy & Foster (2016) experimentally observed rapid evolution of division of labour between two bacterial morphotypes.

### Value of the divided collective

A mucoid morphotype M and a derived dry morphotype D perform distinct roles in collective spreading. Mixed M+D colonies spread in a way neither type achieves alone and produced more cells than either pure M or pure D colonies on day 8. Both pairwise comparisons were P<0.01 after an overall one-way ANOVA of P=0.000957.

This is direct experimental evidence of a collective performance benefit from division of labour. It is an analogue of positive divided-state value, not a literal estimate of SLK Phi.

### Accessibility

The D morphotype repeatedly evolves from M. For one lineage, D carried a two-nucleotide deletion generating a WspC:D hybrid; engineering the same mutation into M reproduced the D morphology and restored collective spreading when mixed with M.

Across independently evolved D morphotypes, single mutations repeatedly affected the Wsp signalling system, alternative mutations generated similar D phenotypes, reverse evolution from D back toward M also occurred, and M backgrounds with the original Wsp route disabled re-evolved a D-like partner through other c-di-GMP routes.

Thus the differentiated collective is reachable through short and redundant mutational routes. This demonstrates accessibility but is not an estimate of the local SLK quantity g0.

### Rare establishment and frequency dependence

Starting M:D frequencies were varied across several orders of magnitude. Populations converged toward approximately 10% M : 90% D, showed strong negative frequency-dependent selection, and allowed each morphotype to outcompete the other when rare.

The divided collective therefore occupies the rare-type-permissive/coexistence route rather than an ecological-stabilization state that blocks a divided type when rare.

The study also reports that D initially increases and collective spreading begins after D approaches a sufficient frequency. That onset of a collective phenotype should not be equated with the SLK positive-frequency critical threshold p_C, because the measured population interaction is negative frequency dependent.

### What this gives SLK

This is the strongest same-system empirical positive control located in the current screen:

collective benefit + short mutational access + rare-type advantage -> rapid realized division of labour.

It shows that value, accessibility and rare-type performance are experimentally separable biological questions and can all be measured in one evolving division-of-labour system.

### Scope boundary

The Pseudomonas system divides labour between genetically distinct members of a colony. The focal SLK manuscript models structural release from a multifunctional architecture. The bacterial system is therefore an EMPIRICAL ANALOGUE / POSITIVE CONTROL, not a DIRECT TEST OF STRUCTURAL SLK TURNOVER.

## Current empirical synthesis

The two strongest anchors play different roles.

Pedicularis rex provides field evidence that a persistent integrated structure experiences strong ecological selection mosaics.

Pseudomonas fluorescens provides experimental evidence that when value, accessibility and rare establishment all align, division of labour can evolve and stabilize rapidly.

Together they make the biological gap precise. The missing evidence is not another example of conflict, specialization, frequency dependence, or path dependence. It is a same-architecture environmental series in which a specified structural alternative is evaluated at all three layers and the first failing layer changes while the resident integrated phenotype remains unchanged.

That missing test remains the empirical target of SLK.
