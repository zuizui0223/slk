# SLK empirical bridge evidence V1

## Purpose

This ledger asks how far existing public or openly described empirical systems connect the three early SLK decision layers in one biological system.

The three layers are architecture/collective value, evolutionary accessibility, and rare establishment. No literature example is promoted to an estimate of Phi, g0, or Delta_R unless the estimand actually matches the SLK definition.

## Result

No public system identified in the current screen closes the full ordered SLK persistence turnover from architecture-value bottleneck to path bottleneck to rare-establishment bottleneck across environments while retaining the same integrated architecture.

Two systems nevertheless provide complementary empirical anchors.

## A. Pedicularis rex — natural selection mosaic around a persistent integrated architecture

### What is directly supported

Sun, Armbruster & Huang (2016) measured floral traits, stigmatic pollen loads, initial seed set, seed predation and final viable seed production across 12–14 field populations. Greater corolla exsertion is associated with greater pollen receipt and also greater seed predation, while seed-predator-mediated selection varies geographically more strongly than the pollinator side.

Sun & Huang (2015) experimentally manipulated water held by the cupular bracts. Across six field populations, background seed predation ranged from 1.36% to 27.42%; draining bracts increased seed predation without changing legitimate pollinator visitation.

Xia, Sun & Liu (2013) found a density-dependent predator effect over two years. Patch density did not significantly affect fruit set or initial seed set, whereas sparse patches had lower final seed set and greater fruit and seed predation. The raw data are publicly archived on Dryad as raw data.xlsx, DOI 10.5061/dryad.6cv06.

### What this gives SLK

P. rex is a strong natural-context anchor: documented functional conflict, persistent integrated morphology, and large geographic/density variation in the antagonist environment.

It supports the premise that an unchanged integrated architecture can occupy substantially different ecological selection contexts.

### What it does not give SLK

The density treatment concerns density of the resident P. rex population. It is not a manipulation of the frequency of a structurally divided alternative.

Resident-density Allee effect is therefore not Delta_R of a divided architecture.

The existing data cannot identify the fitness of a specified divided floral architecture relative to integration (Phi), the local selection gradient along a structural-release path (g0), or invasion fitness of a rare divided architecture (Delta_R). The 2013 Dryad data are valuable for ecological-context analysis but cannot close the SLK establishment gate by reinterpretation.

## B. Pseudomonas fluorescens — experimental positive control when the early gates are permissive

Kim, Levy & Foster (2016) experimentally observed rapid evolution of division of labour between two bacterial morphotypes.

### Value of the divided collective

A mucoid morphotype M and a derived dry morphotype D perform distinct roles in collective spreading. Mixed M+D colonies spread in a way neither type achieves alone and produced more cells than either pure M or pure D colonies on day 8. Both pairwise comparisons were P<0.01 after an overall one-way ANOVA of P=0.000957.

This is direct experimental evidence of a collective performance benefit from division of labour. It is an analogue of positive divided-state value, not a literal estimate of SLK Phi.

### Accessibility

The D morphotype repeatedly evolves from M. For one lineage, D carried a two-nucleotide deletion generating a WspC:D hybrid; engineering the same mutation into M reproduced the D morphology and restored collective spreading when mixed with M.

Across independently evolved D morphotypes, single mutations repeatedly affected the Wsp signalling system, alternative mutations generated similar D phenotypes, reverse evolution from D back toward M also occurred, and M backgrounds with the original Wsp route disabled re-evolved a D-like partner through other c-di-GMP routes.

Thus the differentiated collective is reachable through short and redundant mutational routes. This demonstrates accessibility but is not an estimate of the local SLK quantity g0.

### Rare establishment and frequency dependence

Starting M:D frequencies were varied across several orders of magnitude. Populations converged toward approximately 10% M : 90% D, showed strong negative frequency-dependent selection, and allowed each morphotype to outcompete the other when rare.

The divided collective therefore occupies the rare-type-permissive/coexistence route rather than an ecological-stabilization state that blocks a divided type when rare.

The study also reports that D initially increases and collective spreading begins after D approaches a sufficient frequency. That onset of a collective phenotype should not be equated with the SLK positive-frequency critical threshold p_C, because the measured population interaction is negative frequency dependent.

### What this gives SLK

This is the strongest same-system empirical positive control located in the current screen:

collective benefit + short mutational access + rare-type advantage -> rapid realized division of labour.

It shows that value, accessibility and rare-type performance are experimentally separable biological questions and can all be measured in one evolving division-of-labour system.

### Scope boundary

The Pseudomonas system divides labour between genetically distinct members of a colony. The focal SLK manuscript models structural release from a multifunctional architecture. The bacterial system is therefore an EMPIRICAL ANALOGUE / POSITIVE CONTROL, not a DIRECT TEST OF STRUCTURAL SLK TURNOVER.


## C. Enforced two-function specialization — direct tests of divided-state value and within-divided ecology

### Bacillus subtilis — profitable genetic division of labour

Dragoš et al. (2018) compared a generalist biofilm architecture with engineered specialists that separately produced the two major matrix components EPS and TasA. The genetic specialist pair outperformed the incomplete phenotypic specialization of wild type biofilms. Specialist relative fitness was negatively frequency dependent in vitro and on plant roots, converging to a stable composition of approximately 30% TasA producers; that composition was also the group-productivity maximum.

This is strong experimental evidence that separating two costly functions can increase collective performance and that the resulting specialist composition can be ecologically stabilized.

It is not a direct estimate of SLK Phi or Delta_R because the frequency experiment is competition between the two specialist types after the divided collective has been imposed, not invasion of a whole divided architecture into the generalist resident.

### Pseudomonas aeruginosa — specialization can coexist without profitable division of labour

Mridha & Kümmerli (2022) created the complementary experiment with a generalist wild type producing both siderophores and two engineered specialists producing pyochelin or pyoverdine separately. They manipulated specialist mixing ratios across four iron environments.

The value verdict changed with environment. Specialist mixtures exceeded the generalist in iron-rich medium (t73=12.17, P<0.0001), matched it in plain CAA (t73=1.819, P=0.0730), and under stronger iron limitation fell below the generalist (100 µM bipyridyl: t73=-12.89, P<0.0001; 300 µM: t71=-6.745, P<0.0001).

At the same time, the two specialist types showed negative frequency-dependent relative fitness in every environment. Their estimated stable pyochelin-producer frequencies shifted from 69.80% in iron-rich medium to 38.99%, 22.29%, and 30.09% across the three iron-limited treatments.

The raw workbook is public on Dryad (DOI 10.5061/dryad.2bvq83bs4).

### What the paired comparison contributes

B. subtilis and P. aeruginosa jointly give a particularly clean experimental lesson for SLK:

```text
specialization / complementary roles
does not by itself imply
positive divided-state value.
```

In B. subtilis, enforced genetic division can outperform the generalist-like organization. In P. aeruginosa, the same broad experimental design yields equal or lower productivity under the conditions where the focal functions matter most, even while specialist coexistence is maintained by negative frequency dependence.

Thus collective value and ecological maintenance of the differentiated components are empirically separable. This is close to the SLK distinction between architecture value and population ecology, but it is not the SLK rare-invasion estimand: frequency dependence among two already-present specialists is not Delta_R of the divided architecture relative to the integrated resident.

## D. Bacillus thuringiensis — positive frequency dependence without an SLK establishment failure

Deng et al. (2015) described LM1212, which divides Cry-toxin production and sporulation between terminally differentiated cell types. Competition against a conventional Cry-producing strain was frequency dependent. The conventional strain's fitness increased with its own frequency, and the relative outcome depended strongly on starting composition.

This is useful because it demonstrates that positive-frequency effects can occur around a biological division-of-labour phenotype. It does **not** close the SLK ecological-stabilization gate: LM1212 still outcompeted the conventional Cry producer over a wide range of starting frequencies. The study therefore cannot be cited as evidence that a divided type is selected against when rare.

## E. Solanum rostratum enantiostyly — environment-dependent value of a more divided reproductive organization

Mora-Carrera et al. (2019) experimentally compared monomorphic enantiostyly, in which an individual bears both left- and right-styled flowers, with manipulated dimorphic arrays in which individuals bear only one orientation. The dimorphic treatment gained an outcrossing/reproductive advantage at high floral density, whereas that advantage disappeared or reversed at low density.

This is an unusually close organismal analogue of environment-dependent value for a more divided organization. However, it concerns the distribution of style orientation among individuals, not the pollen-feeding versus pollen-transfer functional conflict represented by heteranthery. It is therefore an adjacent architecture experiment, not a direct test of the focal structural division-of-labour mechanism.

## Cross-system gate matrix

| System | Divided-state value tested? | Accessibility tested? | Frequency / rarity tested? | Direct SLK structural test? | Main role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pedicularis rex | no specified divided alternative | no | resident density only | no | natural selection mosaic under persistent integration |
| Pseudomonas fluorescens M+D | yes, collective benefit | strong analogue: repeated single-mutation access | yes, rare advantage / stable coexistence | no | positive control with all early questions permissive |
| Bacillus subtilis EPS/TasA | yes, divided pair beats generalist-like organization | no | specialist-specialist NFDS | no | positive divided-state value + ecological composition |
| Pseudomonas aeruginosa siderophores | yes, value changes across iron environments | no | specialist-specialist NFDS | no | direct value/ecology separation and public raw data |
| Bacillus thuringiensis LM1212 | partial comparative value | developmental mechanism described | positive-frequency competition | no | shows positive FD near a DoL phenotype but not rare failure |
| Solanum rostratum enantiostyly | yes, density-dependent reproductive value | no | density, not alternative frequency | adjacent only | organismal analogue of environment-dependent architecture value |

The screen therefore still contains **no system that simultaneously measures a specified integrated-versus-divided architecture comparison, the local path away from integration, and the rare invasion performance of that divided architecture across environments**.


## C. Volvocine algae — closest structural bridge for value and developmental access

Volvocine algae provide a closer analogue to the flagship structural problem because division of labour occurs among cell types within one multicellular organization.

Herron et al. (2014) experimentally evolved *Pleodorina starrii* under selection on colony size in still and mixed environments. In the largest colony class, still-environment lines evolved a higher proportion of somatic cells than mixed-environment lines, as expected when motility is more valuable. Yet the smallest 4- and 8-celled colonies retained roughly half of their cells as soma even though comparative and hydrodynamic arguments predict an optimum near zero, and the authors inferred a developmental constraint preventing simultaneous optimization across colony sizes.

Davison et al. (2025) then showed that *Eudorina elegans*, previously treated as undifferentiated, produces somatic-like cells after cold shock. The response can occur directly in stressed cells and can also be carried into offspring development, providing a plausible plastic developmental route toward regulated soma.

A September 2026 bioRxiv preprint by Davison et al. experimentally evolved this route. Repeated cold shock caused 21 of 48 lines (44%) to show more differentiated colonies in baseline conditions; 11 lines changed the proportion of somatic cells within differentiated colonies, and one lineage evolved obligate somatic differentiation. The obligately differentiated lineage remained differentiated after the inducing cold-shock regime had ended, and genomic changes were concentrated in regulatory genes linking stress response and soma-associated expression.

### What this gives SLK

The volvocine work is the closest current empirical bridge for the **path layer**:

- ecological context changes the value of somatic investment;
- developmental organization can prevent apparent optimization;
- an environmentally induced route to somatic differentiation exists;
- repeated environmental exposure can modify that route until differentiation becomes constitutive.

This is stronger than treating developmental accessibility as an abstract mutation-size assumption.

### What it does not give SLK

These studies do not estimate the SLK local gradient `g0` or demonstrate a positive endpoint value separated from the resident state by a measured fitness valley. Nor do they manipulate the population frequency of a newly divided architecture, so they do not estimate `Delta_R`.

Their status is therefore:

```text
STRUCTURAL EMPIRICAL BRIDGE FOR VALUE + DEVELOPMENTAL ACCESS
not a full SLK gate closure
```

The 2026 result is a preprint and should remain labelled as such until peer review.

## Current empirical synthesis

The two strongest anchors play different roles.

Pedicularis rex provides field evidence that a persistent integrated structure experiences strong ecological selection mosaics.

Volvocine algae provide the closest structural bridge showing that ecological value and developmental access/constraint can both be experimentally moved within one germ-soma differentiation system.

Pseudomonas fluorescens provides the strongest same-system positive control showing that collective value, short evolutionary access and rare establishment can all align with rapid division of labour.

Together they make the biological gap precise. The missing evidence is not another example of conflict, specialization, frequency dependence, or path dependence. It is a same-architecture environmental series in which a specified structural alternative is evaluated at all three layers and the first failing layer changes while the resident integrated phenotype remains unchanged.

That missing test remains the empirical target of SLK.
