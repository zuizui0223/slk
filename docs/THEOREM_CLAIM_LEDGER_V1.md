# SLK theorem-claim ledger V1

This ledger separates proved theory, model-specific corollaries, empirical handoffs, unresolved identification requirements, and registered invariants for the flagship chain

```text
L -> R -> Phi=R-K -> accessibility -> invasion -> fixation -> occupancy.
```

Under the registered quadratic partial-release bridge only,

```text
R=sL,
Phi=sL-K.
```

## Claim classes

- **THEOREM** — proved within the declared model class.
- **COROLLARY** — follows from an upstream theorem under additional model structure.
- **DERIVED CONSEQUENCE** — direct algebraic, definitional, or interpolation consequence used for prediction or design; useful, but not a standalone novelty claim.
- **METHOD ADAPTATION** — an established mathematical or numerical device adapted to an SLK measurement problem; novelty, if any, lies in the biological use and claim discipline rather than the device itself.
- **SYNTHESIS / DIAGNOSTIC** — no new component theorem is claimed; the contribution is that a fixed upstream comparison makes otherwise observationally similar outcomes separable by where the measured verdict changes.
- **EMPIRICAL HANDOFF** — quantity may be imported only from an identified empirical analysis.
- **INVARIANT** — two estimands are forced to align under the registered process assumptions.
- **OPEN EMPIRICAL GATE** — required for a biological claim but not yet identified.

## Formal non-equivalence witnesses

`theory/UNIFIED_THRESHOLD_ATLAS_V1.md` gives the master critical-surface atlas in one compatible composite model. `theory/NON_EQUIVALENCE_THEOREM_V1.md` then instantiates all five non-implications inside one convex recovery family:

```text
L>0                    !=> Phi>0
Phi>0                  !=> local accessibility
accessible + Phi>0     !=> rare invasion
rare invasion          !=> reciprocal fixation superiority
absolute fixation advantage !=> greater weak-mutation occupancy.
```

It also proves INV1, so the manuscript must not claim that reciprocal fixation ordering and symmetric weak-mutation occupancy ordering are distinct under the registered process.

## Empirical gates required for a biological application

A complete empirical SLK application requires, in order:

```text
G1  identify a real shared-axis conflict
G2  estimate or bound L on a common fitness scale
G3  define and estimate recoverable release s or R
G4  define and estimate architecture cost K
G5  evaluate Phi
G6  define the local architecture mutation/accessibility neighborhood
G7  estimate population interaction / invasion terms
G8  specify a fixation process and distinguish reciprocal ordering from absolute fixation advantage
G9  specify mutation connectivity if stationary occupancy is claimed.
```

Failure at a later gate does not invalidate an earlier one.

## Anti-overclaim rule

The manuscript must never compress the chain into

```text
Phi>0 -> differentiation evolves.
```

Nor should it claim universal pairwise non-equivalence of every adjacent stage. In particular, `R=sL` under the quadratic bridge, affine threshold shifts, two- and three-frequency coefficient recovery, endpoint invasion limits, and Richardson-type two-scale extrapolation are not standalone mathematical novelty claims. They are transparent derived tools inside the transport framework.

The strongest accurate summary is:

```text
SLK transports one architecture comparison across explicit decision layers: added mechanisms can change the verdict without redefining the upstream comparison, the resulting sign sequence localizes why the same persistent phenotype can arise, and the registered fixation-occupancy process supplies one exact consistency surface.
```
